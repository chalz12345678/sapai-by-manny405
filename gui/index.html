How to play

Goal

Build a team of up to 5 pets. Each turn you shop, then your team fights the other side's team automatically. Win 10 battles to win the game. You start with 10 lives, and losing a battle costs lives: 1 on turns 1–2, 2 on turns 3–4, 3 on turns 5–8, 4 on turns 9–10 and 5 after that. A draw costs nothing.

Shopping

  • You get 10 gold every turn. Unspent gold is lost.
  • Pets and food cost 3 gold. Roll costs 1 gold and refreshes the shop.
  • Freeze keeps a shop item for next turn. It costs nothing.
  • Sell a pet for gold equal to its level (plus any Cake bonus).

Battles

  • The pet at the front (right end of your row) fights the enemy's front pet.
  • Both deal damage equal to their attack (orange) to the other's health (red) at the same time.
  • A pet at 0 health faints and the next pet steps up. The team with pets left wins.
  • Shop stat boosts are permanent unless they say "until end of battle".

Leveling up

  • Buy a copy of a pet you own and drop it on that pet, or drop one of your pets onto its copy.
  • The combined pet keeps the higher attack and higher health of the two, plus 1 of each, and gains 1 experience. 2 experience reaches level 2, 3 more reach level 3 (the max).
  • Higher levels make abilities stronger, and each level-up adds a bonus pet from a higher tier to the shop.

Abilities and perks

  • Most pets have an ability that fires on a trigger, written first: "Faint: …" fires when the pet faints.
  • Some foods give a perk 🍯 shown on the pet's card. A pet holds one perk, and a new one replaces it.
  • Tap any pet or food to read exactly what it does.

Tiers

Pets come in tiers 1 to 6 (the T number on each card). The shop offers higher tiers as turns go on: tier 2 from turn 3, tier 3 from turn 5, tier 4 from turn 7, tier 5 from turn 9 and tier 6 from turn 11. Higher tiers are usually stronger.
